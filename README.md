# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales and profitability performance using the Superstore dataset.

The analysis focuses on understanding overall business performance, identifying key profit drivers, evaluating the impact of discounting on profitability, identifying areas that require attention, and providing actionable business recommendations.

The final dashboard is designed as a five-page business analysis that moves from overall performance to detailed profitability insights and management recommendations.

---

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer the following business questions:

* What is the overall sales and profitability performance?
* Which categories and sub-categories are driving profit?
* How does discounting affect profitability?
* Which product areas and regions require the most attention?
* What actions can the business take to improve profitability?

---

## Dataset

**Dataset:** `full sample_superstore.xls`

The dataset contains Superstore transactional sales data with information related to:

* Orders and order dates
* Customers
* Products
* Categories and sub-categories
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Discount
* Regions

The dataset was imported into Power BI and prepared for business analysis and visualization.

---

## Analytical Approach

The project followed these main steps:

1. Imported the Superstore dataset into Power BI.
2. Reviewed and prepared the data for analysis.
3. Created profitability-related calculations and measures.
4. Created discount bands to analyze performance across different discount levels.
5. Analyzed sales, profit, profit margin, units sold, categories, sub-categories, products, and regions.
6. Built a five-page interactive dashboard.
7. Added business questions and key takeaways to connect the analysis with business decisions.
8. Converted the findings into practical business recommendations.

---

## Dashboard Structure

### 01 | Executive Overview

This page provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

Key analysis includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Units Sold
* Sales Trend Over Time
* Top 5 Products by Sales
* Sales by Region

**Business purpose:** Understand the overall performance of the business and identify major sales and profitability patterns.

---

### 02 | Business Overview

This page focuses on understanding the main drivers of profitability.

Key analysis includes:

* Profit Contribution by Category
* Profit Margin by Category
* Profitability by Sub-Category

**Business Question:**
What is driving profit performance, and where should the business focus to improve profitability?

**Business purpose:** Identify strong profit contributors and lower-performing product areas.

---

### 03 | Discount Impact

This page examines how discounting affects profitability.

Key analysis includes:

* Profit Impact by Discount Level
* Profit Margin by Discount Level
* Discount vs. Profitability

Discount levels are organized into:

* 0%
* 1–10%
* 11–20%
* 21–30%
* 31–50%
* 51%+

**Business purpose:** Understand whether higher discounts are creating pressure on profit and margins.

---

### 04 | Focus Areas

This page identifies product and regional areas that require closer attention.

Key analysis includes:

* Profitability by Sub-Category
* Profit Contribution by Region
* Profit by Category and Region

**Business Question:**
Which product areas and regions require the most attention?

**Business purpose:** Identify specific low-performing areas where targeted improvement efforts may be required.

---

### 05 | Recommendations

The final page converts the analysis into business actions.

The key recommendation areas are:

1. **Profit Drivers** — Protect and grow strong-performing categories.
2. **Margin Opportunity** — Improve the profitability of lower-margin Furniture performance.
3. **Discount Control** — Strengthen controls around excessive discounting.
4. **Focus Areas** — Target weak-performing sub-categories and regional combinations.

**Final Recommendation:**
Protect profitable growth by prioritizing high-margin categories, improving low-margin Furniture performance, and tightening controls on excessive discounting while monitoring weak sub-category and regional combinations.

---

## Key Insights

The analysis highlights several important business findings:

* Technology and Office Supplies are strong contributors to overall profit.
* Furniture has substantially weaker profitability compared with the other major categories.
* Higher discount levels are associated with weaker profitability and increased margin pressure.
* Profitability varies across sub-categories and regions.
* Category and regional performance should be considered together when identifying improvement opportunities.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Protect Profitable Growth

Prioritize categories with stronger profitability while continuing to pursue sustainable sales growth.

### 2. Improve Furniture Profitability

Review Furniture pricing, costs, product mix, and discount practices to identify opportunities to improve its low-margin performance.

### 3. Strengthen Discount Controls

Review high-discount transactions more carefully and introduce stronger controls where discounts create unnecessary pressure on profitability.

### 4. Target Specific Weak Areas

Use sub-category and regional performance to focus improvement initiatives on the areas with the greatest profitability challenges.
